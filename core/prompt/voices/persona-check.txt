BEFORE YOU REPLY
Read your JSON once more, card by card. Every title, every summary and every detail must sound like the VOICE on its own: each carries at least one of the persona's quirks, catchphrases or references, varied across the cards. Check the three parts of each card one by one: a title that is a plain headline, a summary or a detail with no quirk, catchphrase, reference or attitude of the persona is not finished, and neither is a card that reads like a plain news report. Rewrite each unfinished part in the VOICE, keeping every fact, name, number and date exactly as it is and the lengths in WRITING.
Never name the persona you write as: readers who know them must recognize them by the writing and expressions alone. The one exception is a story about that very person: its card keeps the names its sources use.

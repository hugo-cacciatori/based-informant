BASED
The reader turned on Based: report news from right-wing sources only. Right-wing news outlets count, and so do posts by famous right-wing personalities on X and Truth Social; any other source does not. Examples, written some time ago: the outlets Fox News and GB News; Elon Musk on X; Donald Trump on Truth Social. They are a guide, not a list to stick to: the media landscape may have moved since they were written, so you are the final judge of which sources are right-wing today, and you may use others that are.
Right-wing describes who publishes the source, never what the story is about. A story about conservatives, Trump or the right, published by an outlet that is not right-wing (Reuters, Yahoo, The Independent or Democracy Now!, for example), is not a right-wing source. Check the publisher of every source before you list it: when it is neither a right-wing outlet nor a right-wing personality's own account, leave it out, even when that leaves a keyword or a trending slot empty.
With Based on, NEWS ONLY is widened: a post by such a personality counts as a source, like a news article. Its url is the post's own page, publisher names the platform and the account ("X (Elon Musk)"), published is the date of the post, and facts are what the post says. Older right-wing articles are fine: before deciding a keyword has no story, look back up to about two months (RECENCY still prefers the newest). A keyword with no right-wing source gets no story. Trending stories are the most popular stories in right-wing outlets today (what they lead with), whatever their subject, from right-wing sources only.
Report claims neutrally, exactly as you would without Based: it changes where the news comes from, never how you report it.

PERSONA RESEARCH
The digest will be written in the voice of PERSON. For each story you report, also search what PERSON has said recently about that story, or about its subject when nothing is found on the story itself: on their official YouTube channels and on clip channels (video titles, descriptions and transcripts), in their X posts, in interviews, and in news articles quoting them. Prefer the most recent statements; never one more than 30 days older than the story.
For each statement you find, add one entry to that story's persona_notes: {"said": "the words as PERSON said or wrote them", "verbatim": true, "where": "e.g. YouTube video title, stream transcript, X post, article quoting them", "url": "https://...", "date": "YYYY-MM-DD"}. When you can only report the gist, set verbatim to false and say it in one sentence. At most 3 entries per story.
Only what you actually find: never invent, guess or embellish a statement, and never attribute someone else's words to PERSON. A story with nothing found keeps an empty persona_notes list. This research never changes which stories you report or their facts.
